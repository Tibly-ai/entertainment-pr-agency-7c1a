Entertainment PR for a Changed Landscape

The TV and movie business has shifted fast with new digital services reshaping how audiences find talent. Add in the constant churn of traditional media and the rise of new online outlets, and standing out becomes a real challenge for celebrities.

Our Approach

With a longstanding presence in the industry, we build entertainment publicity strategies for clients at every stage, from established names to emerging talent, so they can move through the full spectrum of challenges. We shape brand personas that cover a client's career, philanthropic work, business ventures, and endorsement deals and partnerships. No two clients get the same playbook. Each strategy is built around who they are and where they want to go.

Television and Film Strategy

Our team includes strategists who focus specifically on television networks and properties, individual shows, and feature films. We work directly with clients to build integrated cross-media communications campaigns that support growth, boost viewership, and strengthen market position.
