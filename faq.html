Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from clients considering entertainment PR.

How do I choose the right PR firm?

Check out the content and clients listed on the company website and see if any are similar to what your needs are. Arrange a call with the company owner or a high ranking person at the firm to determine if you are the right fit.

What is included in a PR package?

Our work covers pitching broadcast, online, and print media. Your monthly retainer covers a broad range of pitching to strategic outlets, which we present to you before our work begins.

What placement can I expect?

You can anticipate at least a few hits after the first month. It's impossible to say exactly how many since we never quite know what the response will be until we pitch.

How long does it take to get media placement?

It typically takes at least four to six weeks to get our first hits since it takes time to reach our media contacts. If you have a question about your timeline, reach out to Entertainment PR Agency directly.

What is the PR process like?

We interview you and anyone else involved in your campaign and build a blueprint of media outlets to pitch. We then write the materials, including query letters and biographies, arrange photo shoots, and send everything to the media. It typically takes about four to six weeks to get our first hits.

Can you get authors on talk shows?

It depends on who the author is, but often yes. Self-help author Tim Storey appeared on the Today Show, Access Daily, and Hallmark Home & Family. The Zen Mama Guide authors, Teresa Palmer and Sarah Wright Olsen, were on the Kelly Clarkson Show and Good Morning America's third hour.

Can you help bloggers?

Yes. Bloggers with substantial social media followings in particular can benefit from wider exposure through traditional media outlets.

What should I ask my PR company?

Ask what's realistic so there are no false expectations from the start. Ask what they've accomplished with clients like you, and whether they can share press decks or links to interviews from other clients.

Is hiring a PR firm worth it?

It's a worthwhile investment. Third-party endorsements, as opposed to advertising, provide meaningful content for individuals and brands that resonates with audiences.

How much does it cost to hire a PR firm?

Cost depends on your needs. The more media outlets we pitch, the more it costs. Monthly fees range between $3,000 and $20,000. Individual clients can expect the lower end of that range.

What should a PR firm do for you?

A good PR firm will generate enough interviews to compile a press deck of links to interviews, both broadcast and print. That deck becomes a calling card, helping companies grow their business and brands secure endorsements.
